About Qualitech

Founded in 2002, Qualitech International is a trusted partner for laboratories and manufacturers. Our metrologists deliver fast, fully documented calibrations with ISO 17025 accreditation. This is our official website — explore our services and contact us directly.

Calibration services

Dimensional

Gage blocks, micrometers, callipers, CMM, thread gauges.

Temperature

Thermocouples, RTDs, ovens, thermal chambers, data loggers.

Mass & Force

Precision weights, balances, load cells, torque wrenches.

Electrical

Multimeters, oscilloscopes, power supplies, resistance, HV.

Pressure & Vacuum

Pressure gauges, transmitters, barometers, vacuum sensors.

Laboratory

Pipettes, centrifuges, incubators, pH meters, timers.
